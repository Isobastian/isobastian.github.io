2048IN 3D!

Tap a column to drop the next cube. Line up matching numbers and watch them merge into bigger and bigger values, on a 4×4×4 grid you can spin and view from any angle.

CRYSTALPOWER!

Drag an Emerald Crystal onto a cube: every cube with that number turns to crystal and shatters in a flash of lightning and magic dust.

REACHLEGEND

Chase your best score, climb from 2 to the millions and see how high you can go.

CHAINCOMBOS

Special cubes, chain reactions and satisfying combos. One good drop can set off a whole cascade.

DEEPSTRATEGY

A board that adapts to how you play keeps every run fresh. Simple to start, deep to master.
