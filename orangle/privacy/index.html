Orangle

Privacy Policy

Orangle: 3D Merge Puzzle · Last updated: October 2026
Developer: Sebastian Nicosia · Contact: orangle_support@icloud.com

1. Overview

Orangle is a mobile puzzle game developed by Sebastian Nicosia, available on iPhone and iPad (App Store) and on Android (Google Play). This policy explains what data the app handles, who receives it and what choices you have. It applies to both versions; where they differ, this is stated.

2. Data stored only on your device

Orangle saves the following on your device using Unity's PlayerPrefs:

  • Your current game, score and best score
  • The player name you type in Settings (optional)
  • Crystal balance and purchase status (for example, whether "Remove Ads" was bought)
  • Settings: sound, rotation speed, language and tutorial status
  • The timers for daily rewards and free crystals

This data is never sent to the developer, never shared with third parties and is deleted when you delete the app.

3. In-app purchases

Orangle offers optional in-app purchases (Remove Ads and crystal packs). Payments are processed by Apple through the App Store on iPhone and iPad, and by Google through Google Play on Android. The developer never receives your payment details.

4. Advertising

Orangle shows ads: a banner during play, a full-screen ad after a game over, and optional rewarded videos you can choose to watch for a reward. Ads are delivered through Unity LevelPlay, operated by Unity Technologies, which works with the ad networks ironSource Ads, Unity Ads and Google AdMob (operated by Google).

To show, limit and measure ads and to prevent fraud, these services may collect:

  • The device's advertising identifier: on iPhone and iPad the IDFA, only if you allow tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt; on Android the Android advertising ID (AAID)
  • Device information such as model, operating system, language, screen size and connection type
  • Approximate location at country level, derived from your IP address
  • Ad interactions and in-app activity, such as ads viewed or tapped and app launches
  • App performance data, such as load times
  • Purchase activity within the app, used by the ad network to tailor ads

iPhone and iPad: if you allow tracking, ads may be personalised. If you don't, you still see ads, but they are not personalised using your advertising identifier. You can change this at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.

Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your phone's settings (usually Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads; the exact path depends on the phone). After deletion, ads are no longer personalised using that identifier.

Players in the EU, the UK and Switzerland first see a consent message (Google's certified consent tool, IAB TCF v2) where they can accept, refuse or choose which partners may use their data for personalised ads. Refusing still lets you play; ads are then limited or not personalised. You can change your choice at any time in Orangle → Settings → Privacy choices.

How these companies handle this data is described in the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy and in How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Buying Remove Ads removes the banner and full-screen ads. Rewarded videos stay available only if you choose to watch them.

5. Leaderboard

Orangle has an optional online leaderboard: Game Center (Apple) on iPhone and iPad, and Google Play Games (Google) on Android. If you are signed in to that service, your best score is sent to it together with your Game Center or Play Games profile (player name and avatar), and other players can see it on the leaderboard. The developer only sees the scores and names shown on the leaderboard. You can sign out or change your profile visibility at any time in Game Center (iOS Settings) or in the Google Play Games app. See Apple's privacy policy and Google's privacy policy.

6. Feedback by email

If you send feedback from the app, it opens your email app. The developer then receives your email address and message and uses them only to reply to you.

7. Children

Orangle is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. The game has no registration, login, chat or social features.

8. Your rights

The developer does not keep personal data on any server. To delete the data stored on your device, delete the app. For data collected by the advertising services, you can exercise your rights (access, deletion, objection) through Unity and Google using the policies linked above, and for the leaderboard through Apple or Google. If you are in Switzerland, the EU or the UK, you can also contact the developer with any privacy question.

9. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app changes. The latest version is always available at this address.

10. Contact

For any privacy question: orangle_support@icloud.com
